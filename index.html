K-POP DEMON HUNTERS × ANUA

K-POP / SOCIAL
BBH Singapore · 2026MY ROLE

Social Listening · Creative Ideation · Social Execution

CASE STUDY

Fans already knew how HUNTR/X shine on stage. Gonna Be Glowin’ gave them a way to carry that glow into everyday life, with Anua.

Building on the Anua × KPop Demon Hunters skincare collection, the global campaign centred on a K-pop-style film by music video director Kylie Kang. Everyday moments with the products are cut together with scenes from the movie, and the film is packed with Easter eggs for fans, from HUNTR/X’s manager to the film’s three ahjummas. From there, the story moved into social through creator partnerships and fan activations.

On the social side, the work started with the fandom itself: listening to how fans talk about the film, its characters and its lore, and digging deep into the KPop Demon Hunters IP so every idea felt true to the world they love. That groundwork shaped the creative thinking and, in turn, the social content that brought the campaign to the community.

Social Listening

Tracking fan conversation around KPop Demon Hunters to understand what the community cares about and how they talk about it.

IP Research

Researching the film’s characters, lore and details, so the work spoke the fandom’s language and stayed true to the IP.

Creative Ideation

Contributing ideas for how the campaign could live on social and reward the fans who know the film inside out.

Social Execution

Supporting the social content and activations as the campaign rolled out.

DISNEY / CAMPAIGN

Creative · Production · Localisation

CASE STUDY

Behind every Create 100 piece was a personal story: the Disney moment that first sparked a designer, a musician or an artist. The films set out to capture it, from first inspiration to finished piece.

Created to mark 100 years of Disney storytelling, the campaign brought together names across music, film, fashion and art, with every piece auctioned for Make-A-Wish and Disney opening with a $1 million donation. More than 200 films carried those stories to audiences on social and on Disney Style’s YouTube channel.

Creative

Helping shape each contributor’s story, building the structure of every film around their creative journey, from the first spark of inspiration through development to the finished piece.

Production

Managing and coordinating post-production across all 200+ films, overseeing the visual effects and audio mix so every story felt unmistakably Disney.

Localisation

Translating content spoken in Mandarin Chinese, so these stories could travel beyond the language they were told in.

Deliverables

Over 200 films delivered for social and YouTube.

DISNEY MUNCHLINGS × PRIMARK

MUNCHLINGS / PRIMARK
Primark × Disney · 2023MY ROLE

Creative Direction · Storyboarding · Set Design

CASE STUDY

Munchlings reimagine beloved Disney characters as bite-sized treats. For their arrival at Primark, the goal was to show off just how cute and playful the new plush range could be.

Stop motion brought the toys to life. From Pancake Minnie’s graceful somersaults to Fruit Cup Tigger’s energetic bounces, every movement was designed to let each character’s personality shine through, while hinting at the treat they’re made from.

Creative direction

Co-leading the creative direction, from the first concepts through to developing the chosen route.

Storyboarding

Mapping out each character’s story through movement, inspired by both the food they represent and their individual personality.

Set design

Directing the set design, from the colour palettes and patterns to every prop on the table.

Stop motion

Collaborating on set with the stop-motion team at Picturesmith to bring the vibrant toys to life.

Deliverables

Instagram Reels and Stories, featured on Primark Home’s Instagram.

WESTIN — OWN YOUR MORNINGS

WESTIN / CAMPAIGN
BBH Singapore · 2026MY ROLE

Cross-Functional Leadership · Client Collaboration · Campaign Delivery

3Market rollouts: India, Japan and APEC
100+Deliverables spanning social, Spotify and out-of-home in Japan, India and Singapore
CASE STUDY

Sleep isn’t passive, it’s power. Own Your Mornings gave Westin Hotels & Resorts a bold new point of view: true restoration is what gives guests the power to take on their day.

Carrying that idea from brief to launch meant steering the whole process: building timelines, bringing creative and production teams together, and keeping the client close at every step, so a campaign centred on the Heavenly Bed and Westin’s sleep rituals landed exactly as intended.

The anthem film shows a family owning their morning, with a bold voiceover set against gentle, sunlit moments. From there, the work was adapted for every channel and market, from the hero film and its shorter cuts to print and in-hotel TV, and rolled out across India, Japan and APEC in more than 100 deliverables.

Cross-functional leadership

Overseeing the whole campaign process and keeping every team aligned, from the first brief through production to delivery.

Client collaboration

Partnering closely with the client’s brand team, so the work stayed true to its ambition at every stage.

Campaign delivery

Bringing the rollout to life in India, Japan and APEC, adapting the work for each market and channel.

Deliverables

Over 100 assets spanning social, Spotify and out-of-home in Japan, India and Singapore.

ROGUE

Personal projectMY ROLE

Photography

CASE STUDY

A personal series shot after dark, chasing the moment fire turns into a drawing in the air.

Photographed at night on the sand, the set follows a fire performer through spins, sparks and smoke. Long exposures let each movement leave its own trail of light, so the frames catch what the eye can’t hold: sparks suspended mid-air, smoke lit from within, a figure appearing and disappearing in the glow.
