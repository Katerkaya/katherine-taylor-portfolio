BIO

Katherine Taylor is a British multi-disciplinary creative, artist and storyteller based in Singapore.

She studied Arts and Sciences (BASc) at University College London, an interdisciplinary degree that combined her passion for media and design with psychology and data science. She is a fluent bilingual in English and Mandarin Chinese.

Design, art and creativity have been a big part of Katherine’s upbringing. Ever since she was little, she’s always had a paper and pen (most times more than one) on standby.

That might mean putting together marketing material, coming up with original character designs, or capturing digital content for short-form videos and social reels. Exploring the diversity of storytelling platforms continuously intrigues her, and it inspires her creative approach to every project.

Her experience includes creative campaigns and production at The Walt Disney Company, within the Disney Consumer Products division in London. That work has spanned global campaigns such as Create 100, initiatives like Marvel x The Prince’s Trust, and brand collaborations including Primark x Munchlings and Pleasing x Fantasia.

She has also worked in marketing at Gensler, the world’s leading architectural design firm. Based in the company’s APAC and Middle East Hub in Singapore, she helped produce marketing collateral for clients and content for social media.

Alongside her campaign work, she has experience in photography, interactive media and documentary film from her university years. She also works professionally across the Adobe Creative Suite, including Photoshop, Premiere Pro, Illustrator and InDesign, bringing innovative and captivating visual storytelling to life.
